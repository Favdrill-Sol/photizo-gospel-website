WHO WE ARE

A People Set Apart

Photizo Gospel Mission International is a ministry committed to the preaching of the undiluted Gospel of Jesus Christ.

We exist to transform lives, raise men who are deeply rooted in Christ, and ignite a genuine revival through the Word of God, persistent prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit.
